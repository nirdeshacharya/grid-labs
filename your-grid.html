Where this comes from

Your postcode is sent to postcodes.io to find where it is, and nothing is stored. The substation that serves it comes from SSEN's primary supply-area boundaries, simplified to about 100 m, so a postcode right on a boundary can land in the neighbouring area. Headroom, generation and live outages are fetched from SSEN as the page loads. The fault record, storm sensitivity, low-carbon counts and the look ahead come from entry 02, entry 04 and SSEN's datasets as built on the date below.
