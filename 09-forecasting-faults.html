Can a wind forecast tell you how many faults are coming?

A simple model built from six years of SSEN fault records and the gusts weather models forecast, tested on the three years it never saw.

Entry 02 showed how fault rates climb with wind. This entry asks whether that is enough to look ahead: given tomorrow's forecast gusts, how many high-voltage faults should SSEN's network expect? The answer decides whether the storm fault outlook is worth publishing.
