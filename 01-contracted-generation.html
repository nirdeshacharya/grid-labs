Is more generation contracted than the grid supply points can carry?

A grid supply point is where SSEN's network meets the national transmission system. Generation connected below it that local demand does not use has to flow back up through its transformers. This entry compares what is connected and contracted at each one with the transformers installed there, then tests how much of that is likely to export at once.
