How much Orkney wind does active network management switch off?

Ten years of half-hourly output from the generators Orkney's network holds back when it has to, set against the island's wind and the generators it never holds back.

Orkney makes more electricity than it uses for much of the year, and its links to the Scottish mainland can only carry so much. For more than a decade SSEN has connected extra generators on the understanding that they can be turned down when the network is full, under a control system called active network management (ANM). SSEN publishes their combined output, but not how much they were held back. This entry estimates it.
