How much of a decade's disruption happened on a handful of days?

Every day from 2016 to 2025 is one thin stripe, one row per year. The stronger its colour, the more customer-minutes of supply were lost to high-voltage faults that began that day, across both of SSEN's licence areas.

The five worst days

    Storm names are my addition from public Met Office naming, matched by date. They are not in SSEN's records.

    What it shows

    Entry 02 tests how much of this is wind. On the worst days, SSEN's engineers recorded wind or falling trees as the cause of nearly every fault.

    What this cannot tell you

    • A fault's customer-minutes are counted on the day it began, so a long storm outage lands on one stripe even if repairs ran on for days.
    • Only high-voltage faults are included. Low-voltage faults are more numerous but usually affect fewer customers each.
    • Planned work is left out, as in entry 02. Some records carry dates outside 2016 to 2025 and are not shown.
