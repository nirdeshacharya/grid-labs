Where could home electrification use up substation headroom before upgrades arrive?

SSEN's spare demand capacity at each primary substation, set against its own projections of heat pumps, electric vehicles and new homes in the area each one serves.

Headroom is SSEN's estimate of how much extra demand a substation can take. When it runs out, new connections such as heat pumps or chargers need reinforcement, flexibility contracts or a curtailable connection first. It does not mean power cuts, but it does mean the site has less margin if a transformer fails or is out for maintenance.
