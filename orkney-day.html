About the data

Half-hourly averages from SSEN's Orkney active network management data, loaded from SSEN's open data portal when you pick a day. SSEN labels the columns kW, but the values are MW. "Managed generators" are those the network can turn down when it is full; "other generators" are the rest, excluding small generators under 50 kW. Flow to or from the mainland is generation minus demand. Wind is the hourly wind speed at 100 m over central Orkney Mainland from the Open-Meteo historical weather API (ERA5), drawn between the hours. Times are UTC. The notes on the featured days are worked out from the data on screen; entry 06 has the analysis behind them.

Part of Grid Labs. A personal project, built in my own time from public open data. Not an SSEN product and not SSEN's view.
