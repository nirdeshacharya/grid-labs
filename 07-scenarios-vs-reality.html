Are heat pumps and home chargers arriving as fast as SSEN's 2020 scenarios assumed?

In 2020 SSEN projected heat pumps and home EV chargers year by year under four scenarios. Here are those projections for the end of 2023, against what SSEN later estimated for the same date.
