Entry 05 Finding · posted 21 November 2026

Once you allow for how much overhead line each place has, where does the network fault most?

Ten years of overhead line faults, divided by the length of overhead line they happened on, district by district and feeder by feeder.

Raw fault counts mostly measure size. A district with 7,000 km of overhead line will have more faults than one with 500 km, even if each kilometre is just as reliable. Entry 02 had no way to allow for that. SSEN now publishes the route of every overhead line, so this entry divides by it.
