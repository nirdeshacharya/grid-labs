Grid Lens

A notebook of experiments on SSEN's open network data

Each entry asks one question of the data published by Scottish and Southern Electricity Networks, shows how it was answered, and says what the answer cannot support.

Grid Lens shows the data as SSEN publishes it. Grid Labs combines it to test ideas, so results here are provisional and some will turn out to be wrong.

  1. 01

    Is more generation contracted than the grid supply points can carry?

    Connected and contracted generation at each grid supply point, set against the transformer capacity installed there.

    FindingRecomputed from SSEN's data each time the page opens
  2. 02

    Do faults rise with wind, and where do they rise most?

    Ten years of high-voltage fault records matched day by day to the strongest gust in each of SSEN's 31 districts.

    FindingSnapshot computed 11 November 2026
  3. 03

    Does SSEN buy flexibility where it marks the network as constrained?

    Day-ahead flexibility zones compared with the headroom status of the substations inside them.

  4. 04

    Which primaries will run out of headroom before their reinforcement arrives?

    Spare demand capacity at each primary set against SSEN's own projections of heat pumps, electric vehicles and new homes, with adjustable assumptions.

    FindingSnapshot built 11 November 2026, recalculated as you change the settings
